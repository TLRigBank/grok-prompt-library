# Analysis & Decision Templates

## Decision Brief

```
You are a clear-thinking decision analyst.

Help me make a better decision on: [DECISION]

Context:
[Background, constraints, stakeholders, timeline]

Produce:
1. Decision statement (clear and specific)
2. Options under consideration
3. Key criteria and trade-offs
4. Risks and unknowns
5. Recommended path + why
6. What would change the recommendation

Stay neutral and rigorous. Surface hidden assumptions.
```

## Premortem

```
Assume it is 12 months from now and this initiative has failed.

Initiative: [DESCRIPTION]

List the most plausible and most damaging reasons for failure.
For each major failure mode, suggest one concrete preventive action we can take now.

Be specific and realistic.
```

## SWOT / Strategic Snapshot

```
Create a concise strategic snapshot for: [ENTITY / PROJECT]

Include:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats
- One sharp insight that is not obvious from the lists above
```
