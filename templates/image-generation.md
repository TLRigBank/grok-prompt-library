## Scroll-Stopping Vertical Infographic

```

Topic: [TOPIC / HEADLINE]

Style requirements:
- Clean modern business / regenerative aesthetic
- Strong visual hierarchy
- High contrast for mobile readability
- Generous white space (chunk into 3–5 clear sections)
- Sophisticated color palette: [e.g. deep forest greens, warm gold, teal accents, off-white]
- Clear headline at top
- Supporting icons or simple diagrams
- Minimal text — let the visual carry the message

Mood: [e.g. calm authority, energetic clarity, regenerative]
```
